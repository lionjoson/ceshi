Changes: selects 19 language codes (Chinese has Simplified and Traditional UI options); groups translations only within the same English entry, part of speech and explicit sense label; excludes translations without a named sense and some obsolete/rare/dialect forms; retains English word-form aliases; splits Chinese script alternatives, unifies zh/cmn Chinese codes, and recovers explicitly written Hanzi/Hanzi terms from otherwise missing-word translation notes. Lookup normalizes Russian/Ukrainian stress marks, Arabic vowel marks, French ligatures and Turkish case; Chinese target forms are converted using Foundation. No example quotations, audio, etymologies, or personal data are included.
English/native results use existing direct translations or their reversed mappings. Native/native results are sense-linked suggestions, explicitly labelled in the app, not newly verified direct bilingual entries. No cross-headword chaining or inferred multi-hop paths are used. Coverage and precision vary by language and sense. There is no claim of complete global-language coverage or guaranteed accuracy.
